# Sheldon Mendoza

I am a Trinidadian reggae and soca recording artist and music video director. I run NEXTV and NEXTV AI CREATIONS, an AI media and commercial production studio.

I'm an AI media creator, film producer, and technology developer. I work where AI, media, and digital business meet, building streaming platforms, games, and practical AI products with a Caribbean focus.

My creative career began in 1991, in commercial art, music, and television. I have produced 100+ television shows, directed 100+ music videos, and recorded 100+ songs.
